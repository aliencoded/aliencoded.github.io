<h2>How we handle corrections</h2>

<p>
When Meta Currents publishes something incorrect, we fix it as quickly as possible and note the correction visibly on the article itself. Substantive corrections (errors of fact that affect the reader's understanding) include both the original wording and the corrected wording, plus the date of the change.
</p>

<p>
Minor typographical fixes (spelling, punctuation, broken links) are made silently. Every article also carries a <code>lastUpdated</code> date in its front matter so readers can see when it was last touched.
</p>

<h2>How to flag an error</h2>

<p>
If you spot an inaccuracy in something we've published, please get in touch through any channel listed on the <a href="{{ '/pages/about/' | relative_url }}">About page</a>. Include the article URL and the specific passage at issue.
</p>

<h2>Public corrections log</h2>

<p>
Significant corrections will be logged here once any are issued. As of this writing, no public corrections have been required.
</p>
