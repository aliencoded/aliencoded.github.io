<h1>Editorial Ethics</h1>

<p><em>Last updated: {{ site.time | date: "%B %-d, %Y" }}</em></p>

<h2>Our commitments</h2>

<p>
Meta Currents covers news from multiple political perspectives. Our role is to surface information clearly and let readers form their own views. This page describes how we work, what we will and will not do, and how readers can hold us accountable.
</p>

<h2>Sourcing</h2>

<ul>
  <li>We link to original reporting whenever it exists, so readers can verify claims and read further.</li>
  <li>We attribute facts and quotations to the people, publications, or institutions that produced them.</li>
  <li>Where a source is anonymous, we explain why and what we did to verify the information.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Independence</h2>

<p>
Meta Currents is independent. Our coverage is not influenced by sponsors, advertisers, or the personal political views of contributors. If a story involves a current or prospective sponsor of Meta Currents, that relationship is disclosed in the article.
</p>

<h2>AI-assisted content</h2>

<p>
Some articles are partially constructed using data from large language models, used as a drafting and summarization aid. Every such article is reviewed by a human contributor before publication and carries a note at the bottom indicating LLM assistance. Articles that are 100% human-written do not carry the note.
</p>

<h2>Comments and feedback</h2>

<p>
Reader feedback is welcome. We do not currently host on-site comments. Corrections, suggestions, and tips can be sent through any channel listed on the <a href="{{ '/pages/about/' | relative_url }}">About page</a>.
</p>
