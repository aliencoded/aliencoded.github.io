<h1>Privacy</h1>

<p><em>Last updated: {{ site.time | date: "%B %-d, %Y" }}</em></p>

<h2>Summary</h2>

<p>
Meta Currents is a static website. We do not collect, store, or sell personal information about our readers. The sections below describe the limited data that is processed when you visit.
</p>

<h2>Hosting</h2>

<p>
The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any website, the hosting provider sees your IP address and the page you requested, briefly, to serve the response. We do not have access to or store those server logs.
</p>

<h2>Cookies</h2>

<p>
Meta Currents does not set cookies of its own. Some embedded third-party content may set cookies in your browser when you interact with it — for example, sharing an article via Twitter or following an outbound link to a sponsor's site. Those interactions are governed by the third party's privacy policy, not ours.
</p>

<h2>Analytics</h2>

<p>
The site does not currently run any web analytics. If that changes, we will list the analytics service here and update this date. Any service we choose will be privacy-respecting and will not require a cookie banner.
</p>

<h2>Contact</h2>

<p>
Questions about this policy can be sent through any channel listed on the <a href="{{ '/pages/about/' | relative_url }}">About page</a>.
</p>
