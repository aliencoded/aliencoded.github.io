Military installations across several Gulf states have increased security measures amid continued tensions tied to the ongoing conflict involving Iran.

Defense officials say bases hosting international forces have raised alert levels and expanded monitoring of aerial and maritime activity.

Additional air defense systems have reportedly been repositioned at several facilities across the region.

Authorities say the precautionary measures are intended to guard against drone or missile threats during the current period of instability.

Coalition forces operating in Iraq, Kuwait, and other Gulf states have also adjusted operational procedures to minimize risk to personnel.

Military analysts say the current security posture reflects concerns that regional proxy groups could attempt opportunistic strikes on foreign installations.

Despite the heightened alert levels, officials emphasize that coalition operations in the region continue without major disruption.

Commanders are expected to reassess the security situation regularly as intelligence assessments evolve.
