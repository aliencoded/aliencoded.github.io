Renewable energy stocks have seen increased investor interest as oil prices climb.

Higher fossil fuel costs often improve the relative competitiveness of alternative energy sources such as solar and wind.

Market participants are also anticipating potential policy support aimed at accelerating energy transitions.

While the sector remains sensitive to interest rates, analysts say current conditions could support longer-term growth.

Some investors view the shift as part of a broader structural trend rather than a short-term reaction.
