Gasoline prices across the United States have risen steadily over the past week, reflecting higher global oil prices.

For many households, fuel costs represent an immediate and visible economic pressure.

Experts note that while wages have grown in some sectors, higher transportation costs are offsetting gains for many consumers.

Retailers and logistics companies are also beginning to adjust pricing strategies in response to increased fuel expenses.

If prices continue to rise, economists warn that consumer spending could slow, particularly in lower-income segments.
