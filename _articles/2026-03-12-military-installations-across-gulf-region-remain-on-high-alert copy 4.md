Global financial markets traded cautiously Friday as investors monitored geopolitical developments and movements in energy prices.

Major equity indexes in Europe and Asia showed mixed performance during early trading hours.

Safe-haven assets such as gold saw modest inflows as traders weighed potential geopolitical risks.

Market analysts say investor sentiment remains sensitive to developments that could impact energy markets or global trade routes.

Despite the uncertainty, most major economic indicators remain stable.

Investors are expected to continue monitoring geopolitical developments alongside upcoming economic data releases.
