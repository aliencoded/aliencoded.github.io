Iran has conducted expanded missile exercises in its southern regions, prompting heightened security advisories across the Persian Gulf and neighboring airspace.

Defense analysts report that the exercises involve both surface-to-surface and anti-ship missile systems, with several drills observed near key shipping lanes and military installations.

Neighboring countries have issued notices to civilian aircraft and maritime traffic, advising temporary route adjustments to avoid potential risk zones.

The increased activity comes amid escalating regional tensions, with international observers warning that these exercises may serve as both a deterrent and a demonstration of Iran's strategic capabilities.

Airline and shipping operators are carefully monitoring updates, as slight changes in exercise schedules can affect long-distance flight paths and commercial shipping routes.

Experts caution that while no immediate threat exists to commercial operations, heightened vigilance remains essential until the exercises conclude.
