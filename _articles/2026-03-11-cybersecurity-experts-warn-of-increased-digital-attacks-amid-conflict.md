Cybersecurity firms say attempted digital attacks have increased as geopolitical tensions surrounding Iran continue to escalate.

Researchers report a surge in phishing campaigns and network probing targeting government agencies, infrastructure providers, and media organizations.

Experts say cyber activity often intensifies during geopolitical crises as state-linked groups attempt to gather intelligence or disrupt communications.

Several companies in the energy and transportation sectors have reportedly strengthened monitoring of network activity in response.

Security analysts caution that many of the attempted attacks appear opportunistic rather than highly sophisticated operations.

Nonetheless, organizations are being advised to increase monitoring and ensure that system patches and defensive measures remain up to date.

Governments in several countries have issued alerts warning companies to remain vigilant for cyber threats connected to the broader regional crisis.

Cybersecurity experts say the digital dimension of modern conflicts often unfolds quietly alongside physical military activity.
