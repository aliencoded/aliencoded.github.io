MOUNTAIN VIEW, Calif. — Google filed the third pre-deployment notice under the May 8 voluntary disclosure framework Wednesday afternoon, the company confirmed in a brief public communication, with the notice covering an updated version of its Gemini frontier model and initiating the standard sixty-day review period under the framework's procedures.

The notice, transmitted to the Frontier Model Assurance Council's interim secretariat at approximately 2 p.m. Pacific time, follows Anthropic's March filing (ratified upon the framework's May 8 adoption) and OpenAI's filing this past Saturday. The substantive content of the notice — including the model's capability profile, training-compute scale, and intended-deployment scope — remains confidential under the framework's standard provisions, with the sixty-day review window running through July 19.

Google's corporate communications statement, issued through the company's press channel, characterized the filing as "the operational implementation of the company's commitment to the framework's substantive direction." The statement emphasized that Google had been preparing the notice's substantive content since the framework's preliminary draft circulated in early April and that the company's review-cycle engagement would proceed under the framework's standard procedures.

A senior Google AI-policy official, in a Wednesday-afternoon background briefing for technology reporters, confirmed that the notice covers a successor to the company's currently deployed flagship multimodal model and indicated that the new model "extends the company's reasoning and multimodal capabilities meaningfully" relative to the prior generation. The official did not commit to a specific deployment date but said the company's operational planning was "consistent with a release during the framework's standard sixty-day window."

The Wednesday filing constitutes the third operational test of the framework's mechanics within twelve days of its formal adoption. The Council's interim director, Pavithra Ramaswamy, in a Wednesday-evening statement, characterized the rate of filings as "broadly consistent with the framework's design assumptions" and indicated that the Council's review capacity was being scaled to match the operational pace.

The framework's substantive infrastructure has expanded materially since the May 8 adoption. The Council's deputy director appointment of Dr. Jennifer Park was announced Sunday, with Park scheduled to begin her substantive duties on June 1. The Council's interim board has been preparing for the broader institutional reconstitution that will accompany Park's arrival, with the reconstituted board expected to include nine members representing the signatory companies, academia, civil society, and federal-government counterparts.

Industry analysts said the rate of filings — three within the framework's first twelve days — provides early operational validation of the framework's design and indicates that the major signatory companies are engaging substantively with the disclosure mechanism. A senior analyst at a major U.S. AI policy research organization said the Google filing's timing "essentially completes the early-stage operational test" of the framework's procedural mechanics, with substantive evaluation of the framework's review-quality dimensions to begin in earnest with the Helios mid-cycle review next week.

The Helios pre-deployment cycle's mid-cycle review milestone is scheduled for June 4, with the Council's substantive review-quality assessment for the Anthropic filing to be completed at that point. The OpenAI cycle's mid-cycle milestone follows on June 18, and the Google cycle's milestone falls on July 1. The staggered milestone schedule provides the Council with manageable operational cadence through the first review cycle.

Microsoft and Meta have not yet filed pre-deployment notices under the framework. A senior Microsoft AI-policy official, contacted Wednesday afternoon, said the company's "operational pipeline" remained on track for substantive engagement with the framework during the second half of 2026 but did not commit to a specific filing date. A senior Meta AI-policy official, in a parallel Wednesday-afternoon background statement, said the company's substantive planning was "consistent with framework participation" but similarly declined to commit to a specific timeline.

The substantive interaction between the voluntary framework and the Hennessey-Blackburn statutory framework currently being advanced through the Senate Commerce Committee will be a focus of the framework's August three-month review. The Senate Commerce hearing held Wednesday afternoon provided the first substantive public engagement of senators with the question, with multiple senators pressing witnesses to address what one senator called "the question of whether the statutory framework supplants, complements, or competes with the voluntary framework."

A senior Council official, contacted Wednesday afternoon for background on the Google filing's review-cycle expectations, said the substantive review would proceed under the framework's standard procedures and that the Council's substantive review team had been augmented during the past two weeks to handle the increased filing volume. The official said the Council's review capacity remained adequate to the current and anticipated filing pace.

The Google filing's substantive timing has been observed by industry analysts as consistent with the company's prior product-release cadence. A senior industry analyst at a major U.S. technology research firm, in a Wednesday-evening client note, said the substantive announcement of the model the filing covers could be expected within the framework's standard review window, with the release timing dependent on the Council's substantive review-cycle conclusions.

The framework's substantive operational tempo through the back half of 2026 will substantially shape the broader contours of the U.S. AI-policy environment heading into 2027. The Council's institutional development, the substantive interaction with the federal regulatory framework, and the framework's substantive credibility with the broader civil-society and academic communities will all be substantively shaped by the operational experience of the next sixty days.

The next substantive milestone in the framework's operational cycle will be the Helios mid-cycle review on June 4, with substantive Council communications around that milestone providing the first formal account of the framework's review-quality mechanics in operational practice.
