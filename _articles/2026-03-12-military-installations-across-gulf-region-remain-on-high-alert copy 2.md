Global oil prices moved higher during early trading Friday as investors continued to monitor security developments across the Middle East.

Energy traders cited concerns that any escalation in regional tensions could disrupt shipping routes or energy infrastructure.

Brent crude and U.S. benchmark prices both posted modest gains during overnight trading.

Shipping activity through key maritime chokepoints in the region remains normal, according to industry monitoring groups.

Analysts say markets remain sensitive to geopolitical developments that could affect global supply flows.

Energy markets are expected to remain volatile as traders evaluate both geopolitical risks and broader economic data.
