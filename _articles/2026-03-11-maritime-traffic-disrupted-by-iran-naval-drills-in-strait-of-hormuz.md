Naval drills conducted by Iran in the Strait of Hormuz have temporarily disrupted commercial shipping routes, with some tankers rerouted to avoid proximity to military vessels.

The exercises, which include live-fire drills and coordinated fleet movements, are part of Iran’s ongoing demonstration of its naval capabilities in one of the world's most strategic maritime chokepoints.

International maritime authorities are closely tracking the maneuvers and issuing notices to vessels to maintain safe distances.

Some oil shipments and cargo carriers have delayed departures or altered routes, highlighting the economic impact of even short-term regional military activity.

Analysts note that the Strait of Hormuz remains a critical artery for global energy supplies, and continued tensions may trigger wider precautionary measures for shipping and insurance sectors.

Iranian officials maintain that these drills are routine but emphasize their readiness in the context of rising regional pressures.
