Shipping and freight companies are experiencing higher operating costs as fuel prices continue to rise.

These increases are beginning to ripple through supply chains, potentially affecting the cost of goods.

Companies are evaluating whether to absorb the costs or pass them on to customers.

Economists note that sustained increases in shipping expenses could contribute to broader inflation pressures.

Businesses and policymakers alike are monitoring the situation closely as energy markets remain volatile.
