Airlines are updating flight paths as civil aviation authorities issue expanded advisories related to rising tensions in Iran and neighboring regions.

Updated guidance emphasizes avoiding certain airspace corridors near military installations, missile test zones, and regions with active conflict alerts.

Several international carriers have already implemented adjusted routes for flights connecting Europe, Asia, and the Middle East, with some longer detours over Central Asia or southern Gulf routes.

Aviation safety organizations note that commercial aircraft typically remain well above tactical threats, but precautionary rerouting reduces exposure to potential incidents.

Industry analysts warn that these adjustments may increase fuel consumption and travel time, but consensus remains that safety considerations take priority during volatile geopolitical periods.

Airlines continue to closely monitor updates from the International Civil Aviation Organization (ICAO) and regional authorities.
