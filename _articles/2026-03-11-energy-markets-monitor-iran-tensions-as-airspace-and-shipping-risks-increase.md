Global energy markets are monitoring Iran closely as rising regional tensions pose potential risks to both air and maritime transport routes.

Airspace restrictions and naval exercises have caused temporary adjustments in shipping lanes and airline routes, prompting traders to account for potential delays in oil and gas supply chains.

Analysts highlight that any prolonged disruption in the Strait of Hormuz or Persian Gulf air corridors could impact crude oil prices and delivery schedules.

Market participants remain alert to government advisories and international regulatory updates, emphasizing risk management in planning shipments and contracts.

While immediate disruptions remain limited, the continued escalation in regional security measures underscores the sensitive nature of Iran’s role in global energy logistics.
