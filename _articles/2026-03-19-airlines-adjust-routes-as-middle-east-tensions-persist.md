Major airlines are continuing to reroute flights to avoid sensitive airspace in parts of the Middle East.

These adjustments are increasing flight times and operational costs, particularly for long-haul routes between Europe and Asia.

Airlines say safety remains the top priority, even as rerouting adds logistical complexity.

Passengers may experience delays and higher ticket prices if disruptions persist.

Industry analysts expect these adjustments to remain in place until regional stability improves.
